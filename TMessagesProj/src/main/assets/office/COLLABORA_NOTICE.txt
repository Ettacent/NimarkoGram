The adapted cache keeps decoded local DOCX region canvases rather than server
tiles and compressed deltas. It replaces the screen-dependent bitmap-count
heuristic with explicit byte and count limits, caller-supplied identity checks,
and synchronous detachment/zeroing of evicted canvas backing stores.

The source form of the adapted module is distributed as office-tile-cache.js.
